Our Story

Founded in 2015, Eagle International packers and movers has proudly served customers across India for over a decade. What started as a small family business has grown into one of the most trusted relocation partners in the country.

Built on principles of integrity, reliability, and timeliness, we treat every move as if it were our own. Our commitment is to excellence, ensuring that you can trust us with your family and business moves across India.

Our mission is to make your move completely hassle-free. We handle every detail with precision and care, ensuring your belongings arrive safely while you focus on settling into your new space.

To be India's most trusted and customer-centric moving company, setting industry standards for quality, reliability, and innovation in relocation services.
