Frequently Asked Questions.

How much notice do I need to book?

Minimum 2–3 days recommended, but we accommodate urgent moves wherever possible.

How is the cost calculated?

Based on volume, distance, packing requirements, and any additional services like storage or insurance.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes, we provide regular updates and GPS tracking on most long-distance moves.

Is packing service mandatory?

No, you can choose only transportation if you prefer self‑packing, though we recommend professional packing for fragile items.

Do you provide insurance?

Yes, we offer basic and enhanced insurance options for every move, explained clearly before booking.

What items are not allowed?

Hazardous materials, perishable food, illegal items, and certain high‑value items may have restrictions. Our team will guide you.
